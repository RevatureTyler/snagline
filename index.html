Short clips from live streams, ready to post

SnagLine Clips is a clipping channel. It finds the loudest, funniest and most surprising moments in live streams, cuts them into vertical videos with captions, and shares them on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok.

How the TikTok connection works

SnagLine Clips uses TikTok Login Kit so the account owner can sign in once. It then uses the Content Posting API to send finished clips to that same account as drafts. The account owner reviews each draft in the TikTok app and chooses whether to post it.

What we make

  • Vertical 9:16 clips, usually under a minute.
  • Burned-in captions and a caption that tags the streamer.
  • Posts to our own accounts only: @snaglineclips on Instagram, SnagLine Clips on Facebook.

Questions? Message @snaglineclips on Instagram.

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

What we collect

When the account owner connects TikTok through Login Kit, we receive the TikTok open ID, display name and avatar, and an access token. The website itself does not set cookies, run analytics or ask for any personal details.

How we use it

We use the access token only to upload finished clips to the connected account as drafts. We do not use it to read private messages, follow accounts or collect data about other people.

Where it is stored

Tokens stay on the operator's own computer. We do not sell them, share them, or send them to anyone other than TikTok.

Viewers

People who watch our clips on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or YouTube are covered by those platforms' own privacy policies. We do not receive their personal data.

Your choices

You can disconnect SnagLine Clips at any time in TikTok under Settings, Security and permissions, then Apps and websites. After that, our access token stops working. To ask us to delete anything we hold, message @snaglineclips on Instagram.
